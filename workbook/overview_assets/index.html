2. Were RTs generally faster during inhale than exhale?

No. Raw audio–tactile RTs were 18.47 ms slower during inhale (95% CI 6.96 to 29.98 ms; paired p = .0024). After matched baseline subtraction, Inhale − Exhale was −2.16 ms (95% CI −12.15 to 7.82 ms; p = .664): the corrected phase effect was inconclusive.

Exploratory paired analysis of 40 participants, excluding P011/P033; equal weight across conditions and SOAs, then across participants. Two-sided p values are unadjusted. These are RT differences, not PPS boundary effects.
