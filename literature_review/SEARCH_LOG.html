Search and screening record

Review date: 16 October 2026, Europe/Berlin. Earlier verified material from 15 October was reused. This is a focused narrative review, not a systematic search or a formal consensus estimate.

Question and scope

In human looming-stimulus tactile-response experiments, what supports a sigmoid description of distance-related responses, what challenges it, and what methods justify robust extraction, smoothing, model comparison and parameter uncertainty? The focal outcome is continuous RT, not binary detection probability. A psychometric-function citation therefore cannot by itself validate the RT model.

Included: direct PPS experiments, critical or meta-analytic PPS evidence, relevant methodological papers, and official software/author sources for the actual estimation families. Excluded from the synthesis: unrelated search matches, inaccessible content, unverified bibliography entries, and claims beyond the inspected abstract or code. No participant data were sent to search services.

Tools actually used

The installed multi-source-web-search skill guided broad discovery, exact-title refinement, evidence screening and source inspection. PubMed public searches and indexed records were retrieved over HTTPS; official method and author code was inspected through GitHub. Skills and active tool metadata were checked for Consensus. No Consensus MCP tool/server or general search connector was available. Consensus searches were not executed. Existing consensus.app links in the repository bibliography are historical links, not evidence of a Consensus run in this review.

The evidence table contains 16 distinct works: nine indexed abstracts, six methodological references inspected through software sources, and one PPS model inspected through author code.

Full-text attempts and stopping rule

PMC pages for Varma and Holmes returned browser challenges rather than articles. Earlier PPS full-text attempts had the same limitation. The JMLR paper endpoint and an attempted Columbia author-copy endpoint returned proxy CONNECT 403. The author-copy URL was not verified as an available paper and is not cited as one. Earlier Europe PMC, Crossref and PLOS access was blocked. These responses were not treated as full text; no challenge or network restriction was bypassed.

The review stopped after broad discovery, a refined methodological round, source reading and contrary-evidence checks had produced a coherent limited synthesis, while additional current/full-text lanes were unavailable. This is a practical access stop, not evidence that all relevant papers were found or that the field agrees. Detailed sample sizes, exclusions, risk-of-bias assessments and exact methods from inaccessible papers are deliberately not inferred from abstracts.

Searches ready for Consensus when configured — NOT RUN

  1. In human audiotactile looming paradigms, how consistent is evidence for a sigmoid PPS boundary after accounting for temporal expectancy and sound propagation? Include contrary experiments and meta-analyses.
  2. How do logistic, log-distance and monotonic nonparametric response functions compare for PPS RT profiles, and what sampling supports midpoint and slope identification?
  3. What evidence supports robust RT summaries and smoothing with sparse repeated measurements, and how should model-search uncertainty be evaluated?
  4. What experiments or reviews published since 2020 change the conclusions of Hobeika et al. and Holmes et al.?

Any future results should be deduplicated by DOI/PMID and checked against the original paper before changing the review. Positive results alone should not define the inclusion criteria.
