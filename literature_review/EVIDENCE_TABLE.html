Abstract; methodological audiotactile experiment Challenges obligatory sigmoid/binary-boundary interpretation; supports expectancy controls and a logarithmic alternative Strong conceptual relevance; Study 5 lacks an established equivalent fixed-distance expectancy control

Methodological evidence

ID and reference Evidence inspected Why it is relevant What it does not justify
M1 Ratcliff (1993), Methods for dealing with reaction time outliers. Psychol Bull 114, 510–532. PMID 8272468. Abstract; quantitative methodological comparison Robust summaries can be less sensitive to outliers and cutoffs; motivates sensitivity analysis This study's trim percentages, a universal optimum, removal of all slow valid responses, or equivalence of mean/median estimands
M2 Li & Racine (2007), Nonparametric Econometrics: Theory and Practice. Princeton University Press. statsmodels KernelReg source, including reference Local-constant/local-linear estimation and bandwidth assessment The selected 45-cm bandwidth or a biological sigmoid
M3 Cleveland (1979), Robust locally weighted regression and smoothing scatterplots. JASA 74, 829–836. statsmodels LOWESS source, including reference Local weighted regression as a smoothing family Calling the study's fixed-bandwidth tricube fit the full robust LOWESS procedure
M4 Wahba (1990), Spline Models for Observational Data. SIAM. SciPy spline source, including reference Explicit fit–smoothness tradeoff Equating the study's finite-difference penalty with an exact smoothing spline
M5 Busing (2022), Monotone Regression: A Simple and Fast O(n) PAVA Implementation. J Stat Softw 102, code snippet 1. SciPy isotonic source, including reference Efficient order-constrained least squares Treating imposed monotonicity as discovered evidence or as proof of an S shape
M6 Ritz et al. (2019), Dose-Response Analysis Using R. CRC Press. drc ordinary logistic, fitting options, and citation Parameterized response curves; ordinary logistic differs from log-logistic; shared/fixed parameters Identifiability from five noisy distances or physiological meaning of every fitted midpoint
M7 Efron & Tibshirani (1993), An Introduction to the Bootstrap. Chapman & Hall. SciPy bootstrap source, including reference Resampling-based uncertainty; whole-person resampling preserves this study's repeated-measure unit Nominal unconditional coverage after model search, identification of flat-fit parameters, or eliminating boundary effects
M8 Varma & Simon (2006), Bias in error estimation when using cross-validation for model selection. BMC Bioinformatics 7, 91. PMID 16504092. Abstract; classifier simulations with independent test sets Supports nesting tuning within evaluation; challenges interpreting the optimized tuning error as independent performance Unbiased performance for arbitrary post hoc searches; the paper's classifier numbers are not Study 5 error rates
M9 Steegen et al. (2016), Increasing Transparency Through a Multiverse Analysis. Perspect Psychol Sci 11, 702–712. PMID 27694465. Abstract; methodological proposal/worked example Supports exposing how reasonable processing choices change conclusions Calling a finite post hoc grid exhaustive, preregistered or immune to selection bias
M10 Raue et al. (2009), Structural and practical identifiability analysis of partially observed dynamical models by exploiting the profile likelihood. Bioinformatics 25, 1923–1929. PMID 19505944. Abstract; method for biological dynamical models Distinguishes model fit from how well measurements determine parameters Claiming Study 5 performed profile-likelihood inference, or applying its intervals without a suitable likelihood
M11 Varoquaux et al. (2017), Assessing and tuning brain decoders: Cross-validation, caveats, and guidelines. NeuroImage 145, 166–179. PMID 27989847. Abstract; methodological review, empirical datasets and simulations Qualifies nested validation: performance estimates can remain variable; split strategy matters A quantitative error bound for this RT analysis or treating folds as independent experiments

Claim assessment

Claim Assessment in the inspected source set
Distance-related multisensory RT changes are a reasonable scientific target Supported by direct and related experimental precedent; not unique to PPS mechanisms

Nested evaluation improves on reporting a tuned minimum alone Supported methodological principle, conditional on the actual evaluation pipeline
Current fitted midpoints identify physiological PPS boundaries Unsupported by current Study 5 diagnostics and design controls
This review establishes scientific consensus or submission readiness Unsupported; access and scope are insufficient for that claim
