\section{Discussion}
This study tests whether respiratory phase modulates the representation of peripersonal space and whether breath-coupled visual feedback further modulates that effect. To the extent that breathing phase is shown to be associated with shifts in the PPS sigmoid, the results would extend prior work on bodily-state influences on multisensory integration and add a respiratory-phase channel to existing accounts of how interoceptive signals contribute to spatial representation. We treat the predicted pattern as a working account to be evaluated against the data rather than a claim about its theoretical reach.
